How it works

  1. Merchant creates an invoice (amount, payer, memo, expiry) and shares the link.
  2. Payer approves exactly the invoice amount in tUSD8, then pays — funds go into escrow in the contract.
  3. Merchant clicks Settle to withdraw the tUSD8 to their wallet.
  4. If the invoice expires unpaid, it can no longer be paid. If it is paid but the merchant never settles within 7 days, the payer can Refund in full.
  5. No protocol fee, no owner, no admin, no backend. Every state change is on-chain and verifiable in the explorer.
